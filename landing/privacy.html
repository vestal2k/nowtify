Privacy Policy

Nowtify is a Chrome extension that notifies you when your favorite Twitch streamers go live. This page explains exactly what data it touches and where it goes.

Nowtify has no backend and no analytics. Everything the extension knows about you stays on your device, and the only third party it ever talks to is Twitch's own API, directly from your browser.

Data

What data Nowtify accesses

Twitch login (OAuth)

When you connect your Twitch account, Nowtify uses Chrome's built-in identity API to open Twitch's own login page and receive an access token. Nowtify never sees your Twitch password. The token is stored locally (chrome.storage) and sent only to api.twitch.tv / id.twitch.tv to check which of your streamers are live.

The streamers, groups, and history you add

Stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB. Nowtify never uploads this list anywhere.

Your settings

Refresh interval, sounds, compact mode, and similar preferences are stored via chrome.storage.sync — Chrome's own account-sync mechanism, the same one that syncs your bookmarks. Nowtify's developer never receives it.

Boundaries

What Nowtify does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting
  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No servers operated by Nowtify — there is nothing to breach because there is nothing to store off-device
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties, ever
Permissions

Permissions explained

notifications
To show a desktop notification when a streamer goes live.
storage
To save your streamers, groups, history, and settings locally.
alarms
To periodically check the Twitch API in the background at the interval you choose.
identity
To run the Twitch OAuth login flow described above.
host: api.twitch.tv, id.twitch.tv
The only two hosts Nowtify ever sends requests to.
Your data

Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension removes everything it stored — there is no account to delete anywhere else, because none exists. You can also disconnect Twitch or clear your history at any time from the options page.
