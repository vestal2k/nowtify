Features

Everything the popup actually does

No filler features. This is what ships in the extension today.

Background notifications

A native notification fires the moment one of your streamers goes live.

Twitch team import

Add an entire Twitch team in one click and filter your list by team.

Per-streamer snooze

Pause notifications for one streamer for an hour, a day, or until tomorrow.

Upcoming stream schedule

See when an offline streamer is scheduled to go live next, from Twitch.

History & stats

Every live you've caught, plus your top streamer and game, at a glance.

Live thumbnail preview

Click a live card to see its thumbnail, category, and viewer count.

How it works

Three steps, then it runs itself

01

Connect Twitch

Sign in with your Twitch account via Chrome's built-in OAuth flow. Your credentials never touch Nowtify's code.

02

Add your streamers

Search and add streamers one by one, or import an entire Twitch team in a single click.

03

Get out of the way

The service worker checks Twitch in the background every few minutes and notifies you as soon as it catches someone live.

Privacy

There's nowhere for your data to go.

Nowtify has no server. There's no account with Nowtify, no database, no analytics SDK, just your browser talking directly to Twitch's own API.

  • Your streamer list and history stay in your browser's local storage
  • Your Twitch token is only ever sent to api.twitch.tv / id.twitch.tv
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no third-party scripts
